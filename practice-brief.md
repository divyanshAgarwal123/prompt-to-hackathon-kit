# Practice brief: one useful campus task

Design a ChatGPT-assisted workflow that helps one SIT Pune student complete a campus task that is confusing, repetitive, or time-consuming.

**Time available for a first version:** 60 minutes. **Tools:** free ChatGPT, a laptop or phone, and optional paper mockups. Coding is optional.

Your team must show:

1. A specific student and the task they need to complete.
2. One real input and the workflow's actual output.
3. A normal test and a difficult or missing-information test.
4. One limitation and the next improvement you would make.

For practice, compare ideas on usefulness, feasibility, observed evidence, and honest limits. These are workshop criteria, not the rules of any external hackathon. If you enter a real hackathon, use its published rules and judging rubric instead.

Use public or fictional information. Do not use another student's private data, credentials, or sensitive records.
